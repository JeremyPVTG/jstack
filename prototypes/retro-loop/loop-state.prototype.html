Question. Do the queue states, the reproduce-first rule, the gate and the caps we agreed give the right outcome for the first retro finding (j-build claiming completion without fresh review lenses) when things go wrong: a failure that does not reproduce, a holdout miss, models that disagree, a regression, a changed case, spend that runs out, a second attempt?

Points to check while you click. (1) The gate treats a regression or critical failure on either model as rejected, but a target or holdout miss on only one model as inconclusive. (2) An infrastructure fault gets one free retry per attempt; a second fault records inconclusive. (3) With all 5 control and probe cases at 3 runs per condition, one attempt is 90 runs. At $0.75 a run, two attempts do not fit in $100 unless the second attempt reuses the incumbent's runs.
